FAQS


Q1.Which stream is best after Class 1Oth?
Ans. There is no best stream, it fully depends on your interests and strengths.

Q2.How do I choose the best stream for myself?
Ans.Choose a stream based on your favourite subjects, strengths and careers your are interested in.

Q3.Is Maths compulsory for all careers?
Ans.No, streams like arts, humanities and some other commerce fields do not require maths.

Q4.Can I change my stream after Class 11th?
Ans. Maybe, changing streams may have some limitations, further you can consult your school.

Q5.Are entrance exams necessary after Class 12th?
Ans. Not always. Some careers need entrance exams, while other allow admissions based on Class 12th results.
