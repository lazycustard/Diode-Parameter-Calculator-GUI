ABOUT OUR TEAM


Hackvibes is a pair of school students working together to create a meaningful and helpful digital projects.

Our current project focuses on Career Awareness for Class 10th students helping them understand the streams and the career they sould choose for their future.


"Empowering class 10 with clarity and confidence."

Purpose of Career Awareness Website:
Our purpose of creating this website is to reduce the rate of unemployement happening in our country happening due to bad decisions taken but young minds.

Many students feel confused while chosing a stream after Class 10th. HackVibes aims to reduce this confusion by providing accurate information.

"Our purpose is not to decide careers for students, but to help them choose wisely."



"Two minds.One mission.Infinite Impact."
